**Opa** is an *Opabinia regalis*, a real animal that lived in the seas of the Cambrian period, more than 500
million years ago. Its fossils come from the Burgess Shale in Canada.

It had **five eyes** on top of its head and a single long, flexible arm at the front, ending in a grasping claw.
When its fossils were first presented, the audience reportedly laughed, because it looked too strange to be real.

## Why an Opabinia

Machyon's job is to reach into a list of URLs and bring back exactly what you asked for, without losing any of it.
Opa is that job as an animal: five eyes that [watch every row](../reliability/index.md) from the plan to the
disk, and one long reach that grabs precisely the thing it was sent for.

## The name Machyon

**Mach** is the speed of sound. A **tachyon** is a hypothetical particle that can never travel slower than light.
*Machyon* (MAK-ee-on) is both: built to break the waiting barrier, and never fall back below it.

## Using Opa

Opa and the Machyon wordmark are the project's brand. You're welcome to use the official artwork to refer to
Machyon: in articles, talks, slides and "built with" badges. Please:

- use the files as they are: don't recolour, stretch, rotate or rearrange them;
- use the `-light` versions on light backgrounds and the `-dark` versions on dark ones;
- keep Opa's eyes and claw amber, and give Opa **five eyes**, always;
- don't use the artwork in a way that suggests the project endorses you or your product.

The official files are in the repository, under `docs/assets/brand/`.
